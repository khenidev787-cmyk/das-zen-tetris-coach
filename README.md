# DAS Rhythm Architect 🎮

**A Precision Timing Trainer for Competitive Block-Stacking Enthusiasts**

---

## 🧠 The Core Concept

Where the original project focused on *teaching* the Delayed Auto Shift (DAS) technique through a modified NES Tetris experience, **DAS Rhythm Architect** takes a fundamentally different approach—it transforms your entire keyboard into a musical instrument of precision timing. Instead of simply practicing DAS in a single game context, this tool creates a **universal timing calibration environment** that adapts to any block-stacking game, from modern competitive titles to retro classics.

Think of it as a **metronome for your fingers**—not just showing you where your timing breaks down, but building a personalized neural pathway map of your current input habits and guiding you toward optimal hand-eye-coordination patterns.

---

## 🎯 Why This Exists

Every competitive block-stacker hits the same wall: you know *what* DAS is, you understand the theory, but your fingers simply don't obey. The gap between understanding and execution is where games are won and lost.

**DAS Rhythm Architect** bridges that gap by providing:

- **Real-time latency visualization** – see exactly where your button presses register relative to your intentions
- **Adaptive difficulty curves** – the trainer grows with you, never staying comfortable for long
- **Muscle memory reinforcement loops** – scientifically spaced repetition for physical actions, not just facts
- **Cross-game compatibility** – practice with a universal input mapping that carries over to any game you play

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge—anything released after 2023)
- A physical keyboard with N-key rollover (most gaming keyboards qualify)
- A stable internet connection for cloud features (local mode also available)

### Initial Calibration Process

1. **Open the trainer** and complete the 60-second input baseline test
2. **Follow the visual prompt** – the system will display a series of timing targets
3. **Receive your DAS Fingerprint™** – a unique profile of your current input tendencies
4. **Begin adaptive training** – sessions automatically adjust to your skill level

The first session takes about 10 minutes. Subsequent sessions are fully automated.

## 📈 Training Methodology

The core innovation of **DAS Rhythm Architect** is its **three-stage progressive training framework**:

### Stage 1: Awareness (Days 1-7)
The system introduces you to your own timing patterns. You'll see where you consistently press early or late, how much variance exists in your inputs, and which finger movements cause the most inconsistency.

### Stage 2: Correction (Days 8-21)
Through targeted micro-drills, the trainer helps you consciously adjust your timing. Each session focuses on one specific weakness identified from your previous performance data.

### Stage 3: Automation (Days 22+)
Once your conscious adjustments become stable, the trainer shifts to interference-based drills—forcing you to maintain precision while performing secondary tasks, ensuring your improvements become unconscious habits.
